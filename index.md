# SalaryNspect Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 22, 2026**

SalaryNspect is designed to help users model, review, and maintain records of their compensation.

## Information Entered Into SalaryNspect

Users may enter information such as:

- First name
- Compensation-plan terms
- Production totals
- Employer-reported gross compensation
- Related compensation information

## How Information Is Stored

SalaryNspect stores this information locally on the user's device.

SalaryNspect does not require an account and does not transmit compensation records to the developer or to a SalaryNspect server.

## PDF Exports and Sharing

Users may choose to create and share compensation-audit PDF files.

When a user intentionally selects a sharing option, the PDF is handled through the standard iOS sharing system and any destination selected by the user.

SalaryNspect does not control the privacy practices of third-party services selected by the user.

## Patient or Medical Information

SalaryNspect does not require patient names, veterinary medical records, or other patient-identifying information.

Users should not enter patient-identifying or medical-record information into SalaryNspect.

## Analytics and Advertising

SalaryNspect currently does not include:

- Advertising
- Advertising tracking
- Third-party analytics SDKs

## Data Deletion

Users may remove compensation records within the app.

The **Reset App Data** feature can be used to remove locally stored SalaryNspect data.

Removing SalaryNspect from the device also removes locally stored application data according to normal iOS behavior.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if SalaryNspect's features or data practices change.

Any updated policy will reflect the practices of the current version of SalaryNspect.
